The Challenge

Every school leader already knows this problem. Ed-Heads is the first solution that actually scales.

Research shows students lose an average of 17% of their maths knowledge during every unstructured holiday. You see this in your results data every year. The question is no longer whether the gap exists, it is whether your school is the one that closes it.

You cannot see it happening.

School reports show what happened last term. They do not show you which students are disengaging this week, in which subject, at what point in the curriculum. By the time the exam data arrives, the gap is already measurable.

Teachers cannot prevent it.

Your teachers are available for six hours a day, five days a week, during term time. The other 128 hours, evenings, weekends, holidays, students who are stuck have nowhere to turn. That is where the gaps compound.

Parent communication is manual.

Parent-teacher communication around learning progress is infrequent, inconsistent, and entirely dependent on teacher time. Ed-Heads makes it automatic, every parent, every Friday, without a single teacher action required.

What Your School Gets

Four things your school gains from day one.

📊

School-level data dashboard

A weekly dashboard showing engagement rates, accuracy by subject, class averages, and individual student trends. You see what is working, what is not, and where your teachers should focus. Updated every week, automatically.

📧

Automatic parent progress reports

Every parent at your school receives a plain-language progress report every Friday morning. No teacher involvement required. No manual data entry. Ed-Heads generates and delivers every report automatically.

🤖

24/7 AI coaching for every student

Coach Ed is available to every student at your school, at midnight, on weekends, during holidays. When a student is stuck, Coach Ed explains the reasoning step by step until the concept lands. No additional teacher resource required.

🎯

Free 5-student trial

We offer every school a free 5-student trial with agreed success metrics before any commitment. You see the data, your teachers see the platform, and you make your decision based on results, not promises.

Implementation

From demo to live in under two weeks.

1

Book a demonstration

We walk your senior leadership team through the platform live, including the data dashboard, the parent report, and Coach Ed. Typically 45 minutes. No commitment required.

2

Run a free 5-student trial

We agree on success metrics upfront, engagement rate, accuracy improvement, parent satisfaction. Five students use Ed-Heads for four weeks. We review the data together.

3

School-wide rollout

If the trial data meets the agreed metrics, we move to a school-wide agreement. Institutional pricing is negotiated based on student numbers. Your school is live within one week of agreement.

4

Ongoing support and data

Your weekly data dashboard goes live. Parent reports start arriving every Friday. We provide a dedicated Kenya contact for any support. Your teachers do not need to do anything differently.
