How It Works

How Ed-Heads works, and what it means for your child.

A simple explanation of the three things Ed-Heads does, and what your child's first week looks like.

The Student Experience

What your child actually does on Ed-Heads.

Ed-Heads is designed to be simple enough to use independently. Your child does not need you to set it up or sit with them. It works the way a good study habit should: consistently, without friction, every day.

1

Log in on any device

Ed-Heads works in a standard web browser, no app to download, no software to install. Your child logs in with their username and password from the email you receive after subscribing. Works on a smartphone, tablet, or laptop. Works on Safaricom 4G.

2

Choose a subject

Ed-Heads covers Maths, English, and Science. Your child chooses which subject to work on, or the platform suggests one based on where they are weakest. Sessions take as little as 10 minutes. The platform always picks up exactly where they left off.

3

Answer structured, curriculum-aligned questions

Every question on Ed-Heads is curriculum-aligned, CBC for Grades 1–9, KCSE for Forms 1–4, and IGCSE for students on that track. The questions are designed to build the same skills your child's school is teaching, not a separate curriculum running in parallel.

4

Get instant marking, right or wrong, immediately

There is no waiting. Each answer is marked the moment your child submits it. They see immediately whether they were right or wrong. This instant feedback loop is what builds understanding, not guessing and moving on.

5

If wrong, Coach Ed explains it step by step

When your child gets an answer wrong, Coach Ed activates. It does not just show the right answer, it walks through the exact reasoning: where the error occurred, why the correct method works, and then offers a similar question to confirm the concept has landed. This is the moment that matters. It is also the moment that used to require a tutor at KES 1,500 per hour.

6

Progress saves automatically

Ed-Heads tracks every session, which topics were practised, how many questions were answered, accuracy rate, and improvement over time. Your child can close the browser and come back later. Everything is saved. The platform knows where they are and what they need to do next.

The Parent Experience

What you see, every single Friday.

You do not need to log into the platform or check your child's progress manually. Ed-Heads does that for you, and delivers the summary to your inbox every Friday morning before you've finished your first coffee.

📧

Friday morning: your report arrives

Every Friday morning, Ed-Heads sends you a plain-language progress report by email. No log-in required. You open it, you read it, you know where your child stands.

📊

The report shows:

  • Sessions completed that week
  • Accuracy rate by subject
  • Topics improving vs. topics needing attention
  • Week-on-week trend (up or down)
🔍

One-click access to detail

The report links directly to your child's session history on the platform if you want to see more. You never need to ask your child how school is going. You already know.

💬

If the report raises a concern, or if you just have a question, WhatsApp the Kenya team directly at 254742850588. We are here.

Coach Ed

The coach that never goes home.

What Coach Ed is.

Coach Ed is an AI-powered study assistant built directly into the Ed-Heads platform. It activates automatically when your child answers a question incorrectly.

Unlike a chatbot, Coach Ed is not a general-purpose conversational AI. It is curriculum-specific, subject-specific, and focused entirely on the question your child just got wrong. It explains the correct method in plain language, walks through the reasoning step by step, and then presents a similar question to confirm understanding.

It is available at midnight. It is available on a Sunday. It is available the night before an exam. It is available whenever your child is stuck, not just during school hours.

Curriculum

Aligned to what your child is actually taught.

Ed-Heads does not run a parallel curriculum. Every question on the platform aligns to the specific curriculum your child's school follows, so practice reinforces schoolwork, not replaces it.

CBC

Competency Based Curriculum

  • Grade 1 through Grade 9
  • Maths, English, and Science
  • Questions aligned to CBC learning outcomes
  • Age-appropriate difficulty progression

Ideal for primary school students in Kenyan government and private schools following the CBC framework.

KCSE

Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education

  • Form 1 through Form 4
  • Maths and English
  • Exam-focused question sets
  • Past paper style practice

Designed to help secondary school students build the skills and accuracy required for KCSE examinations.

IGCSE

International General Certificate

  • Years 7–11 (ages 11–16)
  • Maths, English, and Science
  • Over 90% curriculum coverage
  • Cambridge-aligned question types

For students at international schools in Nairobi following the Cambridge IGCSE track. Full alignment documents available on request.
