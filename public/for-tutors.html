Why Tutors Choose Ed-Heads

Three things that change how you teach, and what you earn.

📈

Better student outcomes

Students who practise daily between sessions progress faster than those who only work during tuition hours. Ed-Heads fills the 128 hours every week when you are not there, so when you are there, your sessions are more targeted and more effective.

💰

Passive income, every month

You earn a commission on every month your students remain subscribed, whether you are teaching, on holiday, or asleep. The income continues as long as the subscription continues. You referred once; you earn indefinitely.

🤝

Stronger parent relationships

The weekly Friday report gives you something to discuss with parents directly. You can point to specific topics, specific trends, and specific improvements. Parents who can see data are more engaged, and more likely to continue booking sessions.

How It Works

Four steps from sign-up to income.

1

Sign up as a tutor partner, it's free

Complete the short sign-up form. We verify your details and set up your partner account within 24 hours. You receive a unique referral link and a welcome pack with everything you need to explain Ed-Heads to your students' parents.

2

Share your referral link

Share your unique referral link with your students' parents, via WhatsApp, email, or in person. When a parent subscribes through your link, they are linked to your partner account. You don't need to collect any payment or handle any administration.

3

Parents subscribe

Parents follow your link, review Ed-Heads, and subscribe at KES 2,000/month. The subscription is processed directly, you never handle money. Your commission is tracked automatically from the moment of their first payment.

4

You earn every month they remain subscribed

Your commission is paid to you monthly, via M-Pesa to the number you register. You can track your active referrals and earnings in your partner dashboard. No invoice required. No chasing required. It just arrives.
