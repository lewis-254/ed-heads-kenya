Getting Started

Five steps to your child's first session.

Everything below should take less than 10 minutes. By the end, your child will have completed their first session and you will receive your first Friday report this week.

1

Check your email

Your child's login details, username and password, are being sent to the email address you used when subscribing. They will arrive within one hour. Check your spam folder if you do not see them. The subject line will read: "Your Ed-Heads login details."

2

Log in on any device

Ed-Heads works in any web browser. Open the browser on your child's phone, tablet, or laptop and go to the Ed-Heads website. Enter the username and password from the email. No app download is required.

3

Watch the 3-minute onboarding video

The platform will prompt your child to watch a short onboarding video on their first login. It shows them how to navigate the subjects, how Coach Ed works, and how to track their own progress. It takes 3 minutes and significantly improves first-week engagement.

4

Set a daily study time

Ed-Heads works best with consistency. We recommend agreeing with your child on a specific time each day, 10–15 minutes minimum. Before dinner, after school, or right after breakfast. The habit matters more than the duration. The platform will save their progress and pick up exactly where they left off.

5

Expect your first Friday report

On Friday morning, your weekly progress report will arrive by email. It will show you exactly what your child practised this week, how accurately they performed, and whether they are on an improving trajectory. You do not need to log in or check anything manually. It will just arrive.
