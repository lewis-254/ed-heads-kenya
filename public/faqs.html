The Platform

Yes. Ed-Heads tracks every session, which topics were practised, how many questions were answered, accuracy rate, and improvement over time. Your child can close the browser at any point and return later. The platform always picks up exactly where they left off.

Coach Ed

How the AI coaching feature works, and what it does not do.

Coach Ed is an AI-powered study assistant built into the Ed-Heads platform. It activates automatically when your child gets a question wrong. It does not just show the right answer, it explains the correct method step by step, in plain language, until the concept is understood. It then presents a similar question to confirm understanding.
No. Coach Ed is not a general-purpose conversational AI. It is curriculum-specific, subject-specific, and focused entirely on the question your child just got wrong. It cannot be used for general queries or non-educational conversations. This keeps it focused and effective, and safe for children to use independently.
24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including weekends, public holidays, and school holidays. It is available whenever your child is studying on the Ed-Heads platform. There is no time restriction and no usage limit.
Coach Ed is not designed to replace a tutor, it is designed to fill the 128 hours every week when a tutor is not available. Many families use Ed-Heads alongside tutoring: the tutor provides structured sessions, Coach Ed handles the moments of confusion that arise in between. Many tutors in our partner network say their sessions are more targeted and effective as a result.
