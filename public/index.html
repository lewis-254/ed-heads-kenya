The Problem

The gap that every school sees, but almost none can prevent.

Research is consistent: students lose an average of 17% of their maths knowledge during every unstructured school holiday. It compounds. By the time exam results arrive, the gap is measurable, and two years wide. Schools know it is happening. Until now, there has been almost no way to see it, or stop it.

Learning ends when school does.

Your child's teacher is available for 6 hours a day, 5 days a week, during term. That leaves 128 hours every week, including every evening, every holiday, where a student who is stuck has no one to turn to. That is where gaps begin.

You don't know until the exam.

School reports tell you what happened. They don't tell you what is happening right now, this week, in the subject your child finds hardest. By the time you find out, the gap is already six months deep.

Private tutoring doesn't scale.

The families who can afford weekly tuition give their children a significant advantage. The families who cannot are left hoping the school will catch what they cannot see. Ed-Heads changes that equation.
