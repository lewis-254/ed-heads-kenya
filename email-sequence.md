Hi [First Name],

One week until the Ed-Heads Kenya Early Adopters Webinar.

Before we meet, I wanted to share something that tends to reframe how school leaders think about this.

---

**The 17% problem.**

Research consistently shows that students lose an average of 17% of their maths knowledge over every unstructured school holiday. That's not a rounding error — that's two months of teaching, undone in six weeks of inactivity.

The compounding effect is worse. By Form 3, a student who has experienced four years of standard holiday learning loss can be operating at a level two full years behind where they should be. Their teacher, their school report, and their parents often don't see it until exams reveal it.

**Why schools haven't been able to solve this:**

The traditional response is holiday tuition — expensive, inconsistent, and available only to families who can afford it. Homework packets don't work. Apps without structure don't build the habit. And none of these give the school any visibility into what's actually happening.

**What Ed-Heads does differently:**

- Structured daily practice aligned to the actual syllabus (CBC, KCSE, IGCSE)
- AI coaching that explains wrong answers step by step, available 24/7
- Automatic weekly parent reports — so parents are informed without the school having to generate the communication
- A school dashboard that shows you aggregate data across all enrolled students

On the webinar, I'll show you all of this live — and share the CBC and IGCSE curriculum alignment documents so you can see exactly where the content sits against your specific syllabus.
